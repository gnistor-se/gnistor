★Don’t panic! Get ORGANIZED (pub) ★

Have you thought about organizing with likeminded people? Do you want to take part in planning and organizing political or cultural events? Or do you just want to get to know the different organizations working in Malmö and Lund?

Join us at Smålands Get Organized Pub on Wednesday the 23rd of September! You will have the chance to get to know several different groups from both Lund and Malmö and learn how to get active in them, including some of the committees at Smålands!
