WAR TO THEIR WAR JOINS THE INTERNATIONAL YOUTH STRIKE!

In the last month, people all around the world have mobilised against their governments. In all of these protests the students has been a force to be reckoned with.

The reason for this mobilisation is that our future has been sold by the rich and the political apparatus aligned with their interests. It is on us to stop this doomsday spiral of cutting funds to our education system; accelerating austerity politics; destroying our planet; and financing wars and genocide - all to ensure that the capitalist class can continue to put more money in their pockets.
