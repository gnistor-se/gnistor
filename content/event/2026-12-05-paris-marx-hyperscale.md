Book launch and discussion with local activists against data centers

Paris Marx, host of Tech Won’t Save Us, is visiting Copenhagen and Malmö to present his new book Hyperscale, a critical and urgent account of the AI-fueled data center boom. Paris will be joined by activists who organize against data centers in Denmark and Sweden. Together, we will dedicate the evening to forging international solidarity in the data center resistance movement.

Malmö: Saturday December 5, 15:00-18:00, Möllevångens Folkets Hus (former Kulturbolaget)

Both evenings are moderated by Anders Kjærulff, journalist, radiohost and part of Analogiseringsstyrelsen.

Drinks, snacks, and fika will be provided.

Tell your friends and coworkers! Tickets are limited, so sign up soon.

Accessibility: The venue in Malmö is accessible from ground-level for wheel-chairs. The venue in Copenhagen is unfortunately not accessible.

Presented by data.coop and Abolish AI!
