What happens when a dinner deliberately breaks the rules?

Disconnected is an evening of food, conversation and surprise, where each course explores a different way of disconnecting. Expect familiar ingredients presented in unfamiliar ways, dishes that challenge expectations, and moments that invite you to interact with both the food and the people around you.

The menu has been carefully designed as a complete experience. We won't tell you much more than that.

Join us for an evening of four vegan courses, thoughtful conversation and a dining experience that's a little different from the ordinary.

Important information: 

- Tickets are non-refundable. If you are unable to attend, you are welcome to transfer your ticket to someone else.


- The menu is fully vegan.


- Unfortunately, we cannot accommodate allergies or other dietary requirements. Some dishes contain gluten.


- Your ticket includes four courses and soft drinks.


- Alcoholic beverages will be available to purchase separately during the evening.


- All takings will go to Solidaria Social Center.
