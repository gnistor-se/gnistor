This past winter saw the largest ever immigration enforcement operation in American history carried out in Minnesota. It also saw a historic level of resistance to this operation. Come hear from a participant in this resistance about what happened, how it differed from past social movements and uprisings, and what it means for the future of revolutionary struggle.

The evening will kick off with a ca. 20 mins. presentation/talk, followed by questions & discussion. There'll be a communal vegan dinner afterwards in support of https://chuffed.org/project/alexio-mutual-aid.
